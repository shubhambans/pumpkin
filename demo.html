Try the conversation.

An AI guide helps someone put their wishes into words. Then it drafts notes for a clinician to review against Washington’s POLST form. This is a demo, and nothing here is a medical order.

About this demo

  • Please do not enter real names or anything that could identify a patient. This demo has not had the privacy, security and clinical review that real patient information requires.
  • The guide is an AI assistant, not a clinician. It does not give medical advice or a prognosis.
  • In “Build your own story”, your replies go to Pumpkin’s server and on to Anthropic’s API to write the response. Pumpkin’s server does not store or log them. Ruth’s sample story is scripted and sends nothing.
  • When a live draft is marked as reviewed, the server saves how many fields the reviewer kept or changed and how long the review took. These are numbers only, with no replies or field contents, and they feed the totals shown under the draft.
  • The draft is mapped to the Washington POLST form, REV 04/2021. It is a draft for a clinician to review. It is never a valid medical order.
